The Decay Protocol

Trust is earned every day. If a user goes dormant (no app open for 30 days), their Karma decays by 5 points per day.

Business Value

"This forces users to return purely to protect their 'OG' status. It is significantly cheaper than retargeting ads. We monetize loss aversion."

Why we need this

Solves Sim-Swapping

Currently, switching SIMs has zero penalty. With Karma, throwing away a SIM means throwing away your "OG Status" and access to concert queues and credit lines.

Enables Typhoon Guard

Stakeholders love CSR, but Finance hates bad debt. By lending data only to "Insiders" and "OGs," the risk of non-payment drops to near zero.

Drives Partner Co-Pilot

Merchants want high-value customers. We can now sell ad slots to partners specifically targeting "The OGs"—the most reliable and influential segment.
