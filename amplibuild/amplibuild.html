Who We Are

Built on Expertise, Driven by Excellence

Amplibuild Construction Corp. is a Philippine-registered construction contracting company specializing in residential, civil, and commercial construction projects. We take on projects of varying scale and complexity, from residential renovations to large-scale civil infrastructure.

Founded by engineers and entrepreneurs with deep experience across technology, project management, and construction, we bring a disciplined, systems-oriented approach to every job site. Our track record spans flood control systems in Bataan, road construction in Cavite, government buildings in Valenzuela, and residential projects across Metro Manila.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional value to our clients through the superior execution of construction projects, while continuously enhancing our technical expertise, professional standards, and commitment to excellence.

Our Vision

To be a leading construction company recognized for delivering innovative, sustainable, and high-quality building and infrastructure solutions that exceed client expectations.

Licensed and Professional
Our team includes licensed engineers and certified professionals operating under Philippine construction standards and regulations.
End-to-End Project Execution
From site clearing and demolition through to finishing and handover, we manage the full construction lifecycle so clients deal with one point of accountability.
Nationwide Reach
Projects across Metro Manila, Rizal, Bulacan, Cavite, Laguna, Bataan, and Pampanga. We deploy skilled manpower wherever the job demands.
Skilled Manpower on Demand
We maintain a core team of engineers, foremen, heavy equipment specialists, and skilled workers ready to scale up for large government and commercial contracts.
Technology-Driven Leadership
Our founders bring backgrounds in engineering and enterprise technology, enabling data-driven project management and operational efficiency uncommon in traditional construction firms.
What We Do

Our Services

We deliver across six core construction and infrastructure disciplines, handling both small-scale residential and large-scale civil government projects.

01
Residential Construction and Renovation

Full residential builds, interior and exterior renovations, structural reinforcement, room additions, and finishing works. We handle projects from 80 sqm units to multi-story homes.

02
Civil and Infrastructure Works

Roads, bridges, flood control systems, and drainage infrastructure. We execute government-contracted civil works with the technical manpower and equipment to deliver at scale.

03
Commercial and Institutional Buildings

Commercial kiosks, covered courts, warehouses, and large government buildings. We supply skilled manpower and technical support for institutional construction contracts.

04
Site Development and Demolition

Land clearing, site grading, road layout, excavation, and complete structural demolition with proper debris disposal. We prepare sites for vertical construction efficiently and safely.

05
Electrical and Lighting Systems

Street light installation, retrofitting, pole installation, wiring, fixture mounting, and systems testing. We improve public and commercial lighting infrastructure safely and reliably.

06
Water Management and Drainage

Flood control structures, box culverts, manhole installation, and drainage systems. We deliver reinforced concrete water management infrastructure built for long-term performance.
