How the Growth Engine Works

Growth isn't about rushing; it's about finding the right pace. Systems thinking ensures every part of your business supports the whole.

1. Offer Perk (The Seed)

Launch a voucher. For every customer that redeems it, you earn GB. You aren't just selling; you're building a "Stock" of data fuel.

2. Accumulate GB

As redemptions flow in, your data wallet grows. This is your engine's power. It happens at a steady pace, ensuring stability.

3. Re-Invest (The Harvest)

Spend that GB to Create Ads or Buy Placements. This brings in more customers, starting the loop over again.
