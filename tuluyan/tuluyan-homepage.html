How it works

Set up your property.
Start collecting in minutes.

You bring the guests. Tuluyan handles everything after that — booking, payment, ID verification, and records.

1

Create your property

Tell Tuluyan what type of property you run. Set up your units, pricing, house rules, and availability — the platform walks you through each step.

2

Connect payments & verify guests

Enable GCash, Maya, QR Ph, or cards. Every guest and tenant verifies their ID before a transaction is confirmed. No screenshots, no follow-up, no risk.

3

Share your direct booking link

Each property gets a dedicated page at tuluyan.ph/p/your-property. Share it on Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, or wherever your guests find you.

Trust & Safety

You know exactly who
you're dealing with.

Every booking, tenancy, and agreement comes with a verified identity, a confirmed payment, and a track record.

01
ID-backed verification

Every guest, tenant, and unit owner uploads a valid government ID before their first transaction is confirmed. No ID, no booking.

02
Real-time payments only

No payment screenshots. No "I already sent it." A booking isn't confirmed until the money actually clears. Not before.

03
Every property reviewed

Business registration and valid ID required from all operators. Property details checked before approval. No ghost listings.

04
Reputation follows everyone

A guest who damages a property or a tenant who skips rent won't get a clean slate. Their record follows them across every property on Tuluyan.
