About Tuluyan

Built for the
operators OTAs ignore.

Tuluyan is property management infrastructure for independent operators in the Philippines — the ones running transient houses, boarding rooms, HOAs, and everything in between.

Our mission

Keep more of what
you earn. Run it better.

The average Filipino property operator is a small business owner managing a few units, maybe a whole building. They're handling bookings through Facebook Messenger. Chasing rent over GCash. Tracking maintenance with a notebook. They're keeping 80 cents of every peso while a platform in another country pockets the other 20.

Tuluyan is the platform we built to change that. Not by being another marketplace — but by giving operators the tools to run their own operation cleanly, directly, and profitably.

"If you manage it, Tuluyan handles it. And you keep 97% of what you collect."

We're a small team building property management infrastructure that's actually designed for how Filipino properties work: short-term stays booked over social media, long-term leases managed in person, HOA dues tracked in spreadsheets. We think you deserve better than that.

Why we built this

The problem was obvious.
The solution wasn't.

Property operators in the Philippines have been underserved by software for a long time. The global platforms — Airbnb, Booking.com, Agoda — take 15–20% of every booking and offer no real tools for operators who want to work outside of them. Property management software from other markets is built for entirely different regulatory and payment environments.

What exists locally is fragmented: bookings managed on one app, payments on another, ID verification nowhere. There's no reputation system. No way to know if a guest damaged a property somewhere else. No tools for HOA dues or long-term lease tracking that don't involve a spreadsheet.

We built Tuluyan to bring all of that together — payments, ID verification, reputation, leases, bookings, HOA administration, and a direct booking link you actually own — under one subscription that costs less than what most platforms take in a single booking.

The values behind the platform

You set the prices. You set the rules. You own the guest relationship. Tuluyan is infrastructure — not a marketplace that holds your listings hostage.

Pricing should be obvious

Three line items. No hidden fees. No "contact sales for pricing." No fees that appear after you've committed. What you see on the pricing page is what you pay.

Every operator type matters

HOA operators don't see themselves in "short-term rental" software. Boarding house owners don't see themselves in enterprise property management. We built for all of them.

Trust is structural, not optional

ID verification, real-time payments, and reputation tracking aren't features you turn on for premium plans. They're part of the foundation — on every plan, for every transaction.

Local context, not local flavor

We didn't translate a global product for the Philippines. We built from the ground up for GCash, QR Ph, Philippine property law, HOA structures, and the way leasing actually works here.

Ship fast, fix fast

Who we build for

Every operator type,
actually covered

Tuluyan is not a short-term rental platform with a few extra features bolted on. It's built from scratch for the full spectrum of property operators in the Philippines.

Short-term operators

Transient houses, hotels, resorts, B&Bs, vacation rentals, pension houses, villas, glamping sites, guest houses, lodges, hostels. Every booking from inquiry to checkout — guest ID verified, payment confirmed before check-in, direct booking link you own and share.

Long-term rental operators

Apartments, condominiums, boarding houses, dormitories, co-living spaces, apartelles, studios, townhouses. Lease applications, tenant screening with verified ID, monthly rent collection, deposit and advance tracking — all in one place.

HOA and building administrators

Subdivisions and condominium corporations. Assessment creation, auto-recurring charges, delinquent tracking, late penalty configuration, unit owner verification, building announcements. Most platforms don't acknowledge you exist. We do.

Event venue operators

Event venues, function halls, and spaces that host ticketed events. Venue booking inquiries, hosted event creation with tickets, event window management, and guest count tracking.

Commercial property operators

Offices, retail spaces, warehouses, co-working spaces. The same lease and payment tools used for residential properties, adapted for commercial arrangements.

Rural and agri-tourism operators

Farm stays and campsites that need short-term booking management without the overhead of a hotel system — or a 20% commission to an OTA that doesn't even know where they are.

What sets Tuluyan apart

Pricing model

Subscription + 0.8% transaction fee on the yearly plan. No 15–20% commission that scales with your revenue.

Marketplace

Optional, not required. All tools work for direct bookings. When you're ready to reach new guests publicly, the marketplace is one toggle away.

Payment gateway

Passed through at cost, no markup. The gateway's fee is the gateway's fee — Tuluyan doesn't add on top.

Feature access

Everything included on every plan. No tier splits, no feature unlocks. One subscription covers HOA, long-term, short-term, and events.

Direct booking

Every property gets a page at tuluyan.ph/p/your-property. Share it directly. Keep 97% of what guests pay.

AI assistant

18 built-in skills for setup, messaging, reports, and daily operations. Credit-based so you only pay for what you use.
