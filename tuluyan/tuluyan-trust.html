Step by step

What happens before a booking is confirmed

Every transaction on Tuluyan goes through the same sequence. There are no shortcuts.

1

Guest or tenant submits a request

A guest selects dates, or a tenant applies for a lease. No money moves at this stage — it's just a request.

2

ID verification is required

Before the booking can proceed, the guest uploads a valid government ID and a selfie. This is checked against their account. First-time users must complete this before any booking is accepted.

3

Operator reviews and accepts

The operator sees the request, the guest's verified profile, and their platform reputation. They can accept or decline based on what they see.

4

Payment is collected in full

The guest pays through GCash, Maya, QR Ph, or card. The booking isn't confirmed until payment clears — not pending, not a screenshot: cleared.

5

Booking confirmed. Both sides notified.

The guest receives their booking reference. The operator sees the confirmed reservation on their dashboard. After check-out, both parties can leave a review.

Questions about trust & safety

What counts as a valid government ID?
Tuluyan accepts any Philippine government-issued ID: PhilSys (National ID), SSS, GSIS, PRC ID, Passport, Driver's License, Postal ID, Voter's ID, or UMID. The ID must be current and legible. Expired IDs are not accepted.
Can I block a problematic guest from booking my property again?
Yes. After a stay, you can file an incident report against a guest. This is attached to their verified profile and visible to other operators before they accept a future booking. You can also set your property to decline bookings from flagged profiles automatically. Because all accounts are tied to a verified ID, the guest can't simply create a new account to get around it.
Is guest data kept private from operators?
Operators see the verification status (verified / not verified) and the guest's reputation history. The underlying ID documents are stored securely by Tuluyan and are not directly viewable by operators. Full data is accessible to the platform in the event of a verified dispute.
What happens if a guest disputes a payment?
Because every payment goes through a real gateway (not manual transfer), there is a full transaction record on both sides. Disputes go through the gateway's own resolution process. Tuluyan provides the transaction record and booking documentation to support the operator's case.
How does the reputation system work for HOA and long-term rentals?
The same reputation system applies across all property types. A tenant with late payment history on a boarding house will have that record visible when applying for a condominium unit under a different operator on Tuluyan. The record is attached to the verified identity, not the account.
Can a flagged account create a new account?
Because all accounts are tied to a verified government ID, creating a new account doesn't reset a flag. A different email and phone number won't change the identity check — Tuluyan matches on the ID itself, not the account credentials.
